Football meets three in a row

Know the player.
Claim the square.

Football knowledge meets tic-tac-toe. Nationalities, clubs, leagues and honours turn every square into a new challenge.

English gameplayOffline playiOS

A little football knowledge goes a long way.

01

Two categories. One name.

Every row and column has a category. Name a footballer who fits both to claim their intersection. A wrong answer costs the turn.

02

A proper head-to-head

Challenge the computer at three difficulty levels, or pass one phone between two players. Each footballer can be used only once per match.

03

Settle it in sudden death

Three in a row wins. If a full board has no winning line, take turns finding valid names in a sudden-death category pair.
