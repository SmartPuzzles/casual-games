Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Footy Grid is operated by Ka Yau Cheung. This policy covers the iOS app and its information pages on the Casual Games website.

Saved gameplay

Footy Grid saves match totals, wins, difficulty and category statistics, and haptic preferences on your device so the game can remember your results and settings. A cached full-access status is also stored locally. Offline play does not require a game-account registration.

Online matchmaking is not enabled in this iOS version.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s privacy-messaging service. These services may process device and advertising identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location, app and ad interactions such as ads viewed or tapped, privacy choices, and crash, diagnostic or performance information to provide and measure advertising and manage privacy messages. Available ads depend on your choices and the service’s applicable consent settings. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Where available, open Settings → Ad privacy → Manage choices in the app. You can also manage tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining tracking does not stop gameplay. It does not mean all advertising-related processing stops. Full access removes ad interruptions; purchase verification still uses Apple and RevenueCat.

Purchases and full access

Apple handles the App Store payment; see Apple’s App Store privacy information. RevenueCat processes purchase and entitlement records, a generated app-user identifier, and technical app/device information to check access and restore purchases. The identifier is pseudonymous and can be associated with purchase records. Access checks can occur before a purchase. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and its purchase-data explanation.

Sharing results and contacting support

If you choose Share, the app passes a result summary to the iOS share sheet. The destination you choose handles that shared content under its own rules. If you email support, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you send, so we can respond to the request.

Storage and retention

To remove local game data, use Delete App in iOS, rather than Offload App. This can erase your scores and history. Device backups are managed through your Apple settings. App deletion does not erase Apple or service-provider purchase records, or messages already sent to support.

Support messages and attachments are used to respond to requests and investigate issues. We keep them for as long as needed for those purposes or to meet applicable legal obligations. Apple and service-provider records are handled under the linked provider policies; deleting the app does not automatically delete those records.

Privacy requests and choices

Where applicable law gives you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information, or to object to certain processing, you can contact smartpuzzlesapps@gmail.com and name the app. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it through the available privacy controls or contact us. We may need information to verify and locate your request. Applicable legal requirements and provider handling may affect what can be done. Please do not send passwords or full payment details.

Purchase restoration, troubleshooting and player-data corrections

This website

These privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting a page sends technical request information to the hosting service. GitHub states that it logs visitor IP addresses for security, including for visitors who are not signed in. Other request information may include browser/device details and request times. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

These static pages contain no contact forms or analytics scripts added by us. If you use the appearance switch, the website saves your light or dark preference in your browser. That preference is not sent to us. Following a provider link takes you to a separate website whose own privacy information applies.

International processing

The providers used for advertising, purchases, email and website hosting may process information outside your country, where privacy laws may differ. Their linked policies describe their processing and safeguards. Contact us if you have questions about information associated with the app.
