Support

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Footy Grid is operated by Ka Yau Cheung. For help with the iOS app, use the contact details below.

For help with Footy Grid, email smartpuzzlesapps@gmail.com. Include the app name, app version, iOS version, what happened and the steps that led to it. A screenshot can help; remove unrelated personal information first.

Restore full access

Full access is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The App Store confirmation shows the price for your region.

  1. Check that the device uses the Apple Account that made the purchase and has an internet connection.
  2. Open Settings → Full access, then choose Restore a previous purchase if full access is not showing.
  3. If restoration still fails, send the error text to support. Do not send your Apple password or full payment details.

Restoration checks your access; it does not restore local scores or game history. For store billing or refund questions, use Apple’s purchase help or Report a Problem.

Ads, rewards and privacy

If an ad cannot load, try again later. A reward is applied only when the app receives an earned-reward result. Closing without earning, a failed load or an unavailable ad does not grant the extra benefit.

If Ad privacy appears in Settings, choose Manage choices. A Retry ad setup or Check ad privacy option may appear after an error. Tracking permission is managed separately in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Apple’s tracking-permission guide and the app’s privacy policy.

Report a player-data error

Send the row and column categories and the player you tried, the expected answer, your app version, and a reliable source link if available. Current club checks have partial coverage; career and performance facts may use an earlier snapshot. Reports are reviewed for possible corrections; this page does not promise an update date.

Local progress and deleting the app

Gameplay records are saved on the device. Before deleting the app, be aware that local progress can be lost. Offload App keeps documents and data; Delete App removes the app and its local data. See Apple’s storage guide. Removing the app is separate from purchase restoration or a privacy request.

For a privacy or data request, email the same support address and name the app.

About this support website

This is a static GitHub Pages website. GitHub processes technical requests and logs visitor IP addresses for security. Website hosting is separate from the iOS app’s advertising and purchase services. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the app and website privacy policy.
