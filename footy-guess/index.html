01

Follow the clue file

Reveal clues one by one, then name the footballer in as few clues as you can. Three wrong guesses end the round.

02

Beat the clock

In Interrogation, ask yes/no questions or build your own from the available categories. Narrow the suspects and make your guess before time runs out.

03

A fresh daily case

Come back for a daily clue puzzle and build a streak. Choose a difficulty for more cases at your own pace.
